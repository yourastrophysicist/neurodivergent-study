Universal Neurodivergent Study Strategy

1. The 15-Minute Micro-Sprint

Tell yourself you will only study for 15 minutes. Starting is 90% of executive friction!

2. Energy Wave Match

High energy = deep dives. Low energy = 2-minute maintenance dose without guilt.

3. "80% is Exam-Ready" Rule

Exams never require 100% perfection. Stop when daily micro-goals are complete!

4. Teach a Peer (Protégé Effect)

Explaining a concept to others cements it into your own long-term memory.
