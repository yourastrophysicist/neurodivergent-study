Master Blueprint: Science, Language & Neurodivergence

A personalized blueprint for navigating cognitive energy, perfectionism, motivation, and emotional regulation across science & language.

1. ADHD Strategy (Executive Friction & Dopamine)

  • The 15-Minute Micro-Sprint: Starting is 90% of the battle. Tell yourself: "I will only study for 15 minutes."
  • Dopamine Chaining: Collect XP badges instantly as you study to keep the brain engaged.
  • Novelty Rotation: Rotate study formats (audio synthesis while walking, formula matrix on laptop).

2. Bipolar Energy Wave Strategy

  • High-Energy / Hypomanic Waves: Ride the momentum! Do deep concept dives, write essays, or build new flashcard decks.
  • Low-Energy / Depressive Waves: Switch to **🛋️ 2-Min Dose**. Listen to 1 audio clip or flip 3 cards. Zero guilt, zero streak loss.
  • Preventing Burnout: Cap study sprints even when feeling hyper-energetic so mood stays stable.

3. OCPD Perfectionism & Circuit Breaker

  • The "80% is Exam-Ready" Rule: Exams do NOT require 100% accuracy. Perfectionism causes analysis paralysis.
  • Structured Formula Tables: Use predictable rule tables to satisfy the need for logical order.
  • Strict Scope Boundaries: Once your daily 4 micro-goals are checked, stop studying! Avoid endless organizing.

4. BPD Emotional Balance & Rejection Safety

  • Rejection-Free Community: The Community Exchange is built as a supportive safe space for mutual peer learning (Protégé Effect).
  • Neutral Error Framing: Quiz mistakes are just data points to guide your memory, never a reflection of your worth.
  • Daily Affirmations: Use the built-in positive reinforcement banner on hard days.
