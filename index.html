Executive Function Scaffolding

Built for Real Neurodivergent Lives

Standard planners assume linear energy and daily memory. Neuro-Spices is designed from the ground up to eliminate planner guilt.

Guilt-Free and Undated

Took a multi-week break? Zero wasted pages. Jump straight back in without feeling guilty about empty calendar dates or broken streaks.

Low Energy Survival Mode

Having an overwhelming or low-energy burnout day? Switch to the minimal 5 percent energy spread: 1 North Star task, comfort list, and zero pressure.

Spoon Budgeting

Map out your tasks based on actual energy spoons. Allocate 1-spoon quick tasks vs 4-spoon deep work tasks so you do not overcommit.

Dopamine Menu and Reset

Keep your brain stimulated with 2-minute starters, side dishes, and dessert rewards, alongside a 5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding exercise.

ADHD Tax Tracker
